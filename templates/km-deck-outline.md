## Slides

| # | Slide title | Key visual / content | Talk track (1–2 lines) | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Topic, date, presenter | — | 0:30 |
| 2 | Why this, why now | The trigger: release feature / review finding / decision pending | Anchor it to our project, not SAP theory | 1:30 |
| 3 | What you'll be able to do after this | The 3 learning objectives, verbatim | Set the bar | 1:00 |
| 4 | The concept in one picture | Single diagram of the whole idea | The mental model everything else hangs on | 2:00 |
| 5–8 | Core walkthrough | One idea per slide, build up the diagram from slide 4 | Keep each slide to one takeaway | 12:00 |
| 9 | Demo setup | What system, what we'll build/show, what "done" looks like | Say the fallback if it breaks | 1:00 |
| 10 | DEMO | (live) — steps listed in `km-session-template.md` §5 | Narrate the *why* of each step, not the clicks | 12:00 |
| 11 | Before / after on our code | A real (anonymized) example from our repo or reviews | Make it concrete to this team | 2:00 |
| 12 | When NOT to use this | The boundaries / anti-patterns | Prevents cargo-culting | 1:30 |
| 13 | Cheat sheet | The screenshot-and-keep slide | Read it aloud once | 1:00 |
| 14 | How this applies to your current work item | 2 discussion prompts | Open the floor | 6:00 |
| 15 | Links + next session teaser | SAP notes/docs, repo paths, next topic | — | 1:00 |
